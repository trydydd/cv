## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** A design-literate viewer would clock this as AI-made within seconds — not because it's ugly (it's disciplined and handsome) but because it's a textbook specimen of the saturated editorial-typographic lane: uppercase tracked mono kicker over a display serif name, hairline rules, monochromatic plum restraint, whisper hovers. It is precisely the "sterile lab minimalism" DESIGN.md's rework exists to correct, and none of the rework has landed: zero ochre, zero Besley italic asides, no specimen numbering, and a footer "colophon" that contains no colophon. The human voice the brand promises exists in exactly three gallery ledes, the 404, and `llms.txt` — everywhere a first-time visitor isn't.

**Deterministic scan:** Near-clean, corroborating that the problem is absence of personality, not presence of slop. CLI detector: zero findings across `src/pages`, `src/components`, `src/layouts`, `src/styles/global.css`, and the built HTML (gallery excluded). Browser-injected detector found two genuine warnings: `all-caps-body` on the landing kicker (66 characters of uppercase at `src/pages/index.astro:14` — the detector agreeing with the design review that the kicker is overloaded) and `border-accent-on-rounded` on `.metadata-panel` (`src/styles/global.css:385`, border-top 2px + border-radius 2px — cosmetic). Two additional hits (`gradient-text`, `theater-slop-phrase`) were proven false positives via a blank control page: the detector was scanning its own injected script. No horizontal overflow at 390px on any page; zero console errors.

**Visual overlays:** No user-visible overlay was possible — this critique ran in a remote headless container with no browser a human can see. Fallback signal: headless injection succeeded (preflight passed on all four pages) and structured console output was captured instead.

## Overall Impression

The site is a well-built, accessible, honest implementation of the *old* brand — and a 0% implementation of the new one. Craft is real: the detector found almost nothing, focus states and reduced-motion are genuine, the routing promise ("route by intent, then disappear") is kept. But every device the reworked DESIGN.md prescribes is missing, the ledger's human-authored copy reads more machine-generated than some of the model output it frames, and the single worst defect sits at the conversion moment: a joke email address that looks like an unfilled placeholder. The biggest opportunity is simply to cash the docs in.

## What's Working

- **The routing architecture delivers the core promise.** Two options, the primary visually heavier, resume one click from anywhere.
- **Accessibility hygiene is real, not performed.** Skip link, visible plum focus rings (keyboard-verified in screenshots), `aria-current`, reduced-motion guard, semantic ledger, mobile reflow that keeps the PDF reachable. The detector's clean sweep backs this up.
- **The three LFM ledger ledes and the 404 prove the voice works.** "Kept as an honest look at the capability floor, not a site bug" is exactly the humor-of-plain-fact DESIGN.md asks for — the template already exists in the codebase.

Why: the technical visitor's first credibility question — "was this one hand-edited?" — goes unanswered on the page that most needs the answer.
